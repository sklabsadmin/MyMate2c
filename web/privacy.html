Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mythos Live ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile and web application (the "App" or "Service").

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email, in which case we receive a private relay address from Apple.
  • Purchase information: If you buy Mythos Coins, we receive a record of the purchase (product, date, and a transaction identifier) from Apple via our payment processor, RevenueCat. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Conversation data: Messages you send and receive within the App, used to provide and improve the chat experience.
  • Usage data: General information about how you interact with the App, such as feature usage and session activity.
  • Device information: Basic technical information such as device type, operating system, and app version, used for diagnostics and compatibility.

How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's core chat functionality.
  • To keep your conversation history associated with your account across sessions and devices.
  • To improve, personalize, and troubleshoot the Service.
  • To communicate with you about support requests you initiate.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to power parts of the App, including AI language providers for generating responses, Apple and Google for optional sign-in, and RevenueCat for verifying in-app purchases. These providers process data only as needed to provide their service to us and are bound by their own privacy and security practices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with service providers as necessary to operate the App, or where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain conversation and account data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service.

Deleting Your Account

If you are signed in, you can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your conversation history, your profile, and your Mythos Coins, and, for Sign in with Apple, revokes the app's access to your Apple account. We keep an anonymous record of past purchases for accounting and tax purposes; it is no longer linked to you. You can also ask us to delete your data by email (see Contact Us below).

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will indicate the "Last updated" date at the top of this page whenever changes are made.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at admin@sklabs.us.
