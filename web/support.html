Mythos Live Support

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Need help with Mythos Live? Email us at admin@sklabs.us and we will get back to you, usually within two business days. You can also reach us from the app: Settings > Contact Support.

Mythos Coins

  • You earn free Mythos Coins every day and as you chat, and you can spend them on gifts for the personalities you talk to.
  • Optional coin packs can be bought in the app. Coins have no cash value, are not cryptocurrency, and cannot be transferred, redeemed, or withdrawn.
  • Coins bought in the iOS app usually appear in your balance within a few seconds. If they don't, close and reopen the coin wallet. If they are still missing after a few minutes, email us with the date of the purchase and the order ID from your Apple receipt.
  • Until you sign in, coins are stored on your device. Sign in with Apple or Google (Settings > Account) to keep them safe and use them on your other devices. Use the same sign-in method on every device: an Apple sign-in and a Google sign-in are separate accounts.

Refunds

Purchases in the iOS app are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Your conversations and data

  • You can clear your conversation history at any time in Settings.
  • To delete your account and its data, open Settings > Account > Delete Account in the app while signed in.
  • You can also ask us to delete the data we hold about you by emailing admin@sklabs.us.
